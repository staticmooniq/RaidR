Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Google Play Games PC different from BlueStacks or Nox?

Google Play Games PC is Google's official desktop platform developed in direct partnership with Supercell. Unlike third-party emulators that emulate a bloated Android phone OS with heavy ARM instruction translation and bundled ad trackers, Google Play Games runs an official, native x86_64 PC build of Clash of Clans with direct DirectX 12 hardware acceleration and full Supercell TOS compliance.

Do I need Bluestacks or any Android emulator?

No. RaidR connects directly to the official Google Play Games PC application for Windows. It requires zero emulator installation.

Can I use my PC for other tasks while RaidR runs?

Yes. RaidR dispatches input through native Windows messages rather than taking control of your physical mouse. You can minimize or cover the game window and work freely.

How does the bot know whether to pay with Gold or Elixir?

RaidR reads the exact button positions in the multi-select wall bar and checks the price text for redness. If one resource is depleted, it automatically switches to the affordable resource without stopping.

Is RaidR really 100% free?

Yes. RaidR is free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no paid tiers, VIP keys, or hidden subscriptions.
